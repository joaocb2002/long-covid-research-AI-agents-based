# How the information was processed

## Collection and selection

The patient supplied 159 unique Reddit posts selected for symptom overlap. Additional discovery used search-engine results restricted to Reddit, Reddit community searches and relevant links within threads. r/covidlonghaulers was the main starting community; other communities were included when relevant.

Search terms translated the clinical description into ordinary language, including fight-or-flight, inner restlessness, pounding without a fast pulse, waking symptoms, internal vibration and head pressure. Psychiatric-label terms were also retrieval hooks, not diagnoses. Criteria were refined during exploration; this was not a prospectively registered systematic review or a validated similarity score.

Titles and snippets identified candidates. Exact-page review included accessible comments, which could justify retention even when the opening post was a weak match. Generic, unrelated or duplicate material was screened out; useful contrasting accounts were sometimes retained. Search rankings and the chosen phrases affected what was found. The saved logs identify at least 248 considered posts, but not every early search hit. Sixty-five additional posts were collected.

Ordinary page controls were used to expand replies. Access barriers were recorded; no bypass was part of the protocol. Partial capture does not mean every historical comment was recovered. Source records preserve text available at collection, rather than a forensic copy of all Reddit data.

## Rules, organisation and extraction

Stage 1 checked and froze the collection after the initial gathering. Stage 2 defined 74 descriptive concepts and allowed field values. Stage 3 separated source passages and comments, attached source-local author labels, and retained locators. A single comment could contain several separately recorded details, sometimes called coded statements. Here, coding means assigning a category label, not programming. For example, a sentence about headaches after COVID and relief with a medicine can contribute separate details about a symptom, timing and treatment response.

Stage 4 used independent extraction passes, source-grounded reconciliation and technical/semantic audits. Symptoms, chronology, treatments, tests, diagnostic claims, hypotheses and function/safety statements were recorded separately. Presence, denial and uncertainty were not interchangeable. Similar symptoms did not establish the same diagnosis or cause.

Five intended-use classifications were retained: qualified comparative use, descriptive use, context only, requires adjudication and excluded. These involved qualitative judgments, not an objective numerical grading instrument. Records awaiting a use decision were not silently converted into approved comparative evidence. The readable results describe non-excluded reports; the machine tables also retain the historical comparison rule for checking.

Stage 5 checked the combined records, their relationships, duplicated material and restrictions. Stage 6 produced descriptive tables and case comparisons. A later bounded extension counted accounts within posts and six symptom combinations. Stage 7 produced the readable reports.

## Counting

For each symptom, repeated comments by the same labelled account within one post count once. The two documented cross-post account clusters are merged in the symptom count selected for the reading documents. Complete cross-post identity matching is not established.

Combinations require the same account within the same post; statements by different contributors are never pooled into one person. The same-message subset requires one text unit. Neither condition establishes simultaneous symptoms, a shared cause or complete similarity to the clinical case. The six combinations were fixed before their aggregation, but after earlier findings were known; they remain exploratory.

The symptom tables include an explicit-only check alongside the original explicit-plus-limited-inference rule. Treatment tables count posts with coded name matches, not patients treated or successful responses. The finite treatment-name groups can miss synonyms and ambiguous formulations.

## This compact release

Records are projected from the final master without re-extraction or regrading. Narrative excerpts, raw names, notes, doses and private paths are omitted. Local author/unit relationships, coding fields and the inputs needed for counting are retained. Treatment-group memberships preserve the earlier name-matching decisions; the recount does not re-extract treatment names from prose.

The included historical extraction protocol is a dated method record. Its old file paths, clinical-anchor version and planned gates are not instructions to run this package or evidence that every planned condition was achieved. The present reproduction script uses only this folder.

The project concerns the same patient and core clinical presentation throughout. The written history was expanded and clarified over time; the included patient-provided PDF is the current reference for case writing. The earlier analytical documents remain identified in provenance rather than being retrospectively replaced. Publication decisions, medical review and any future change to the analysis remain the medical team's responsibility under CARE and the applicable research/privacy arrangements.
