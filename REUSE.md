# Reuse and data boundaries

This repository is a compact research release intended to support medical review and case-report writing. Public access is not evidence of ethical approval, clinician validation or permission to republish all underlying Reddit content.

- The identified clinical-history PDF is included at the project owner's explicit request. It contains his personal health information. Do not imply that it is an anonymous clinical record or redistribute it under an assumed open-data licence.
- Reddit usernames and verbatim third-party post/comment bodies are not included. Public post URLs and source identifiers remain traceable and can lead to their authors. Coded health reports are not guaranteed anonymous merely because names were removed.
- The full narrative and historical processing archive remains separately preserved. Availability of that archive is not promised as unrestricted public access or automatic access on request.
- A blanket licence over the clinical PDF, Reddit material or all derived data is not asserted. Contact the project owner and obtain the relevant permissions before further redistribution or sensitive reuse.
- The counting script is supplied for inspection and reproduction of this release. An explicit general software/data licence can be added after ownership and reuse terms are settled; none is silently imposed on third-party content here.
- Do not interpret counts as population prevalence, confirmed distinct patients, evidence of treatment efficacy, a shared mechanism or proof for/against a psychiatric diagnosis.

CARE structures reporting of the central clinical case. It does not replace any separate ethics, privacy, platform-use or consent assessment for online reports. See [CARE](https://www.care-statement.org/checklist) and [research using social media](https://www.bath.ac.uk/guides/research-using-social-media-and-other-online-data/).
