Long COVID: AI-agent-led evidence processing

A compact guide to selected online accounts, their processing and the descriptive findings.

Most collection, extraction, reconciliation and technical checking was executed by AI agents under the patient's direction. The medical team is responsible for interpretation, validation and case-report writing under the CARE guidelines.

Clinical history and treatment course: the patient-provided reference for case writing. The project concerns the same patient and core clinical presentation throughout. The written history was expanded and clarified over time; its document history is preserved.

The collection in plain numbers

The patient supplied 159 unique posts. Further searches added 65, giving 224 collected posts with accessible comments. Thirteen posts used to test the procedure were kept outside numerical analysis, leaving 211.

The dataset contains 14,961 separately recorded details about symptoms, timing, treatments, tests and other experiences. These are sometimes called coded statements: here, "coded" means given a category label, not computer programming. For example, "My headaches began after COVID, and medicine X helped" can supply separate details about a symptom, its timing and a treatment response. One comment can supply several details. The total is not a count of comments or people.

Selection sought symptom overlap and sometimes retained useful contrasts. Counts describe selected reports, not population prevalence or a clinically verified patient cohort.

Seven processing stages

StagePurpose
1. PreserveCheck and freeze collected sources and access notes.
2. DefineWrite categories and recording rules.
3. OrganiseSeparate messages, local authors and source references.
4. ExtractRun separate AI passes, reconcile and audit.
5. CheckReview combined-data consistency and restrictions.
6. AnalyseCount features and combinations and compare with the clinical description.
7. PresentProduce readable reports and supporting tables.

Medical review was historically called Stage 8. It remains separate from these seven execution stages.

What the effort does—and does not—show

The historical review recorded 293.51 hours of main-agent task duration and 1,213 subagent task identities through 11 November 2026. Task duration includes waiting and I/O; it is not measured GPU or inference time.

The effort and large historical archive produced concise descriptive findings, not a large independent clinical cohort. AI interpretation can be wrong, and separate agents can share errors. There is no benchmark demonstrating an advantage over a simpler workflow.

The matching JSON catalogues and tables of labelled details are in the same folders. The data guide explains their fields. AI methods explains the model records and limitations.

From the package root, run python3 Reproduce/rebuild_tables.py. It uses the standard library and needs no Internet, LLM or original archive. A successful check reproduces calculations from annotations, not a fresh AI extraction or clinical validation.

Original author labels work mainly within posts. Only documented cross-post links are merged. A complete distinct-patient total is not established. The narrative archive remains separate; quotations and other third-party free text are not redistributed in this compact release.
